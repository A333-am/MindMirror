🌿 Types of Breathing Exercises

🌬️ Deep Breathing

Inhale → Exhale Slowly

Breathe in slowly through your nose. Now breathe out gently.

⬜ Box Breathing

4 – 4 – 4 – 4

Inhale for 4 seconds → hold for 4 → exhale for 4 → hold for 4.

🌿 4-7-8 Breathing

4 – 7 – 8

Inhale for 4 seconds → hold for 7 → slowly exhale for 8.

🍃 Slow Breathing

4 – 6

Breathe in gently for 4 seconds, then breathe out slowly for 6 seconds.

🫶 Belly Breathing

Diaphragmatic Breathing

Breathe in gently and allow your belly to expand as you inhale.
